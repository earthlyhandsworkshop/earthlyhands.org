## Certification as written

Right side of the page:

I do hereby certify that the above named persons were captains in my district at the time of the treaty made at Dancing Rabbit creek, on the 27th Sept. and that a list of their names was furnished by me to Armstrong, when called on, for the purpose of allowing the additional half section, as provided for in the treaty.

Done at the agency, on the 7th September 1831.

Greenwood Leflore  
Chief of Northwest District Choctaw

Teste W. Ward Agent C. N.
