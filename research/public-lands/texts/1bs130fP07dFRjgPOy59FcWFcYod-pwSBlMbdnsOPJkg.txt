This is unusually important for attribution discipline. SOURCE OBSERVATION: the numeric category phrase is graphically aligned to the John Coleman row, while the same row’s prose explicitly names Curtis Collins, described as `a white man`, and his family as cultivating the place.

The source itself gives a reason to keep graphic row association distinct from person-level attribution.

This is more than a general caution about uncertainty. The first may be built from this form; the second cannot inherit names automatically from graphic alignment. This control remains useful if the larger emancipation flare fails because it governs any reconstruction of the return’s population and property grammar.
