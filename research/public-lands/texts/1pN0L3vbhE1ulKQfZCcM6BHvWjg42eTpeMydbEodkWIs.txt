Finding


Page 81 places explicit family-distribution language beside the printed family-count machinery.


On `PL:p81:r15`, the row carries an `Entire No. of Family` value of `1` while the locality prose separately says `rest of family over Mississippi.` On `PL:p81:r17`, the row carries an `Entire No. of Family` value of `7` while the locality prose says `Balance of family over Mississippi.` Between them, `PL:p81:r16` carries family total `18` and the source-written note `includes his son-in-law.`


The source therefore does not permit the workshop to treat the printed family figure as a self-explanatory, universally exhaustive kin or household count. At minimum, these three rows show that the recorder could place additional information about family extent, distribution, or an included relational person in prose outside the printed family column. The exact administrative counting rule remains open.
