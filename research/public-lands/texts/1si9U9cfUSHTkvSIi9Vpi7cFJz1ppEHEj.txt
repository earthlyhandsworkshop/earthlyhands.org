## What this face is

Handwritten lined sheet. Stamped **24323**. Date at upper left: **Sep 30th 1830**. Heading, as far as the surviving ink allows: the following are the names of Treaty Captains.

The top and the right edge are eaten. Rows 1–2 and the first heading words are the worst.

This is the same stamped page Holdfast already marked on OHC_2409–2411. OHC_2413 is another capture of that page, not a second list.

OHC_2408 said “thirty Captains.” This face has numbered rows **1–30**. That match is noted. It does not make the later typed cover into this handwritten body.
