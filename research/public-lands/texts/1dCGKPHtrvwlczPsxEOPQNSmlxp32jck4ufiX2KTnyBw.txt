FIELDGLASS CONTINUATION — 2026-09-06 — OPENING-PAGE CATEGORY SUBTOTAL


A closer direct-source read of the OHC_2418 face materially deepens the opening page. Page 39 itself contains seven explicit smaller annotations reading “1 white]” and one explicit “1 Slave]”. The seven “1 white]” occurrences align with the rows for Lewis Bryan, Daniel Boone, Henry Garvin, George Murphey, George Johnston, John Anderson, and William Langphier. The placement is not uniform. This is source-visible evidence that the category notation is not confined to one printed column. It weakens any assumption that the printed form supplied a dedicated white/slave field and strengthens the possibility that these are layered annotations added around the principal return entries. The source still does not establish when, by whom, or under what instruction they were added.
