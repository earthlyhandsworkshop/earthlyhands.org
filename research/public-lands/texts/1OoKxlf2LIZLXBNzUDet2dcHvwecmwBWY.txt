A Copy of American State Papers, Public Lands, Volume 7, 1834 - 1835

(Choctaw - Treaty of 1830 Document 24324)

This volume begins with a list of Captains in Nitachachee's district in 1830. It then lists the names of Indians owning farms in Mississippi, number of persons in the family, the size, location and quality of the land and other information.

All pages were filmed regardless of degree of legibility.

Typed contents cover. Clean reading. No names transcribed beyond the header description. Document stamp/number 24324 visible in the parenthetical.
