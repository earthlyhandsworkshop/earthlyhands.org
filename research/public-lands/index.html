How to use this work

Read a jacket, check the historical source, and keep disagreements visible. A jacket is a working reading with correction history, not an institutional determination. Some entries are provisional or still await independent review.

The public working text is ours to offer. Institutional scan copies from separate holdings are not bulk-republished without checking the rights of those specific representations; the Library of Congress source is directly accessible above.

Internal Drive IDs in working notes are provenance handles and do not promise anonymous access. Send proposed corrections with a page and source reference to workshop@earthlyhands.org.
