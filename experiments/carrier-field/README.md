## Why this is a new specimen

Do not replace or revise `Follow the Carrier`.

That specimen has its own successful physics: a full source/listening piece, progressive reading, persistent earned state, and a historical field that changes as source beats are reached.

Carrier Field starts from a different visual proposition.

The document / examination table stays held above.

A small collapsible tray lives at the bottom of the viewport. Opening it must not make the reader lose the document. Closing it returns the tray to a small handle rather than resetting state.

The tray carries small named bodies, carrier spans, earned stops, and open seams.

## First rule

**NO BODY CROSSES A GAP WITHOUT A CARRIER.**

A named person, object, proposition, case, letter, stamp, notebook, patent, or other durable thing may be shown only at the strength actually earned.

A line means something has been carried.

A missing line is a legitimate state.

A later administrative join can carry a relation, but it must not silently backfill an earlier missing span.

## First mechanics test

The first visible test uses the current Robert Bell comparator seam:

`1830 treaty framework → [OPEN / MISSING CARRIER] → 1837–38 adjudicative body → later administrative carriers`

This does not establish two different men. It protects the fact that the present workshop body does not yet earn a free continuous carrier back to 1830.

The bottom tray also includes Mary Caroline Atkinson and MCR 879 to test three different kinds of duration:

- a human witness occurrence;
- an administrative person / comparator body with an open seam;
- a case body that persists through later administrative states.
