Rough Edge · durable working scene · 23 Oct 1900

Mary Caroline Atkinson

Muskogee, Indian Territory — October 23, 1900

A hearing already in progress.

COMMISSION
What is your name?

MARY
Mary Caroline Atkinson.

COMMISSION
What is your age?

MARY
I am 38.

COMMISSION
What is your post-office address?

MARY
Durant, Indian Territory.

Question.
Answer.
Question.
Answer.
The room finds its rhythm.

COMMISSION
What is your father’s name?

MARY
Robert Bell.

The name lands without music.

Continue the hearing

COMMISSION
Was his name ever on any of the tribal rolls of the Choctaw Nation?

MARY
Yes sir it was on the roll of the Mississippi Choctaws, the treaty of 1830. It was registered in Mississippi.

COMMISSION
Have you any evidence of the fact that he was a recognized member of the Choctaw tribe of Indians at that time?

MARY
Of course I don’t know anything only what I have heard them say. His name is there and I have often heard him speak of his right here.

The Commission keeps narrowing.

COMMISSION
Did any of your ancestors ever receive or claim any land in Mississippi as beneficiaries under the 14th article of the treaty of 1830?

MARY
Not that I know of.

COMMISSION
You state that your father never received any land in Mississippi—

MARY
I said not that I knew of.

The Commission asks if she has anything further.

MARY
Nothing more only that my father was a part Choctaw. That is all the grounds I have.

There may be a whole lot more but I don’t know what it is.

Of course I don’t want to say anything only what I know to be so.

wit bench · unresolved on purpose

Where the joke may actually live

The government does not simply “lose Robert once.” It keeps producing different record populations in which Robert’s visibility changes, then later treats one of those populations with great confidence while deciding whether a living family belongs to its historical Robert.

Possible comic rhythm:

They have Robert.
Then they do not have Robert in the book they are checking.
Then another government body has Robert again.
Then in 1900 they return to the Robert-less book and say, with perfect administrative confidence: no Robert here.

[crickets]

That is a working comic construction, not a historical proposition. The folio’s job is to let every piece be opened until the wit earns itself.
