Earthly Hands Article XIV · architectural study

Visual experiment · unfinished architecture

The old room remains.

A treaty promise stands. Later governments build machinery around determining who may pass through it.

This is not a complete legal history. It is a sectional study in how the ground changes without erasing the original room.

1830 · treaty chamber

Promise before credential.

The original text establishes a path for remaining in Mississippi, land, residence, grant, and retained Choctaw privilege. It does not contain the later descendant-identity tribunal.

Treaty of Dancing Rabbit Creek · Article XIV

Remain. Signify. Reside. Receive the grant. Do not lose the privilege of a Choctaw citizen.

The later machine will ask different questions. This room stays visible so those later questions do not become the treaty itself.

origin node · treaty promise

1898 · administrative addition

Identity becomes a jurisdiction.

The Commission first articulates a descendant-proof theory; Congress then authorizes it to determine the identity of Choctaw Indians claiming Article XIV rights.

Commission interpretation → Curtis Act §21

The question shifts from what the treaty promises to who this applicant is in relation to it.

Interpretation and statute stay separate. The January 1898 Commission theory is not silently rewritten as treaty text.

new room · identity determination

1900 · gate added

Identification is no longer the end state.

A duly identified Mississippi Choctaw may settle in the Choctaw-Chickasaw country and, upon proof of bona fide settlement, move toward enrollment and allotment.

Identification → settlement → proof → enrollment

A legal identity determination becomes a door into another sequence.

This is one of the moments when an older treaty relation becomes operationalized as a later administrative credential.

gate state · not final state

1901 · proposed wing

Designed. Not opened.

The negotiated agreement developed a more elaborate Mississippi-Choctaw architecture, but the source-controlled implementation path stops at the congressional ratification gate.

unbuilt legal architecture

A doorway drawn into a wall that never became governing law.

The proposed structure matters because it shows what federal and tribal negotiators were trying to build. It does not govern MCR 879 merely because it existed on paper.

1902 · evidentiary architecture

Credential does not become origin.

Final §41 makes full-blood status and descent from an Article XIV patentee express evidentiary lanes while keeping identification, settlement, proof, enrollment, and approval distinct.

Rule of evidence · later machinery

Patent / scrip can carry evidence of an earlier treaty state. They are not the source of the treaty right.

Later Interior construction is especially important here: documentary residue may function as proof without becoming the origin node.

evidence carrier · not origin node

One bridge still missing

MCR 879 was reviewed. The authority bridge remains unrecovered.

The case-local path is source-controlled: Commission transmittal, Indian Affairs recommendation, Interior review and affirmance. What remains open is the contemporaneous authority or administrative construction that made that merits-review lane proper.
