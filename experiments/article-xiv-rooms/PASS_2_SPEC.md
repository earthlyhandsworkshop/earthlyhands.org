# Article XIV rooms — pass 2 — tight interpretation / implementation spec

**Status:** next bounded build pass  
**Parent experiment:** `/experiments/article-xiv-rooms/`  
**Ground:** Treaty implementation / paired Robert study  
**Passage:** True North / Mapping Grounds / EarthlyHands.org  
**Build posture:** refine, deepen, do not widen

## Pass-2 aim

Refine the existing architectural experiment without changing its basic character.

This pass has three jobs:

1. repair the 1901 unfinished-room / door composition;
2. make the linework exact enough to carry meaning rather than atmosphere;
3. make the 1830 treaty structure **visibly persist** through later administrative additions.

No new chronology is required. No new Bell identity claim is permitted. No system-wide website grammar is declared.

## Governing visual proposition

> **The old room remains. Later machinery is built around it, across it, and against it — but not in its place.**

Pass 1 states this conceptually.  
Pass 2 must make it structurally visible.

The visitor should be able to feel, before reading the explanatory prose, that later administrative states are additions to an older legal room rather than replacements for it.

---

# I. DO NOT REDESIGN THE PIECE

Preserve the qualities that already caught:

- dark sectional / architectural atmosphere;
- monumental display type at earned moments;
- demanding workshop / legal diction;
- long pauses and real negative space;
- 1901 as unbuilt architecture rather than a normal timeline event;
- MCR 879 and Wash / Jane / Sallie as separate chambers;
- pale Bell-case register as a distinct later material state;
- `The machine changes. The source distinctions remain.` as a full-screen-scale ending;
- ordinary prose large enough to read comfortably on a phone without zooming.

This is a **refinement + one conceptual deepening** pass.

Do not polish it toward generic web design.

---

# II. PRIMARY REPAIR — 1901 UNBUILT ROOM

## Existing strength

The door is one of the strongest visual objects in the experiment.

Preserve:

- narrow door proportion;
- dashed construction language;
- strong central solitude;
- unbuilt / unrealized feeling;
- absence of a literal “NOT RATIFIED” infographic treatment.

## Existing defect to test

On the phone screenshot, the explanatory text appears visually stranded near the top while the door occupies a very large lower field. Some construction lines risk reading as responsive-layout fallout rather than deliberate architecture.

Pass 2 must distinguish:

> **beautiful emptiness**  
> from  
> **accidental emptiness**

## Required composition

Treat the 1901 chamber as three controlled vertical bands.

### Band A — explanation

Keep together:

- `1901 · proposed wing`
- `Designed. Not opened.`
- one short explanation of the congressional-ratification gate

The group should read as one architectural annotation block, not text abandoned above a large object.

### Band B — unbuilt chamber

This is the primary visual field.

It should contain:

- dashed room boundary;
- the door;
- a small `unbuilt legal architecture` annotation;
- restrained projected / construction lines.

The door should have clear optical ownership of this field.

### Band C — unrealized continuation

Allow substantial empty ground below the door.

But give that emptiness one deliberate signal:

- a projected wall;
- a construction datum;
- a dashed unfinished extension;
- or the persistent treaty member passing through.

Use one or two marks only.

The lower field must read as **space that was planned but never became inhabitable**.

## Door constraints

Do not:

- enlarge it into an illustration;
- add animation merely for drama;
- add a glowing threshold;
- add a literal lock, barrier, X, or prohibition symbol;
- decorate it into a theatrical stage prop.

The power is that it is simply **a door inside architecture that never acquired governing force**.

# III. LINE DISCIPLINE

Pass 2 treats linework as language.

Every visible architectural line must have a job.

Before keeping any line, classify it as one of:

1. **original treaty structure**
2. **later built administrative structure**
3. **construction / drawing datum**
4. **proposed / unrealized structure**
5. **case-local government layer**
6. **none of the above — remove or reduce**

## Line hierarchy

### A. Original treaty structure
- continuous;
- visually stable across rooms;
- quiet but persistent;
- must not become a progress bar or timeline.

### B. Built administrative structure
- continuous, but may begin only where the later machinery actually enters;
- may cross or abut the treaty structure;
- cannot visually erase the older member.

### C. Construction / drawing datum
- thinnest and lowest-contrast line family;
- used sparingly;
- should help the page feel drawn rather than decorated.

### D. Proposed / unrealized structure
- dashed;
- reserved for architecture that was designed / proposed but did not become operative;
- strongest use is the 1901 wing.

### E. Government characterization layer
- not required to become interactive in pass 2;
- if visible, it must remain visually distinguishable from the source architecture.

## Start / stop rule

No line may stop merely because a container ends.

If a line terminates, the termination should read as one of:

- structural end;
- legal / administrative addition boundary;
- unrealized extension;
- deliberate clipping at an evidentiary edge.

Otherwise continue it or remove it.

## Mobile line check

At phone width:

- no line should appear to be a random hairline from another section;
- no dashed line should look like a rendering glitch;
- no structural member should collide with body copy;
- line contrast must survive real phone brightness without overpowering text.

---

# IV. NEW CONCEPTUAL MOVE — PERSISTENT TREATY MEMBER

This is the one new idea pass 2 is allowed to add.

## Purpose

Make the statement `The old room remains` literally true in the visual architecture.

## Form

Choose **one** persistent structural member.

Preferred forms:

- one vertical datum / wall edge;
- one continuous chamber spine;
- one boundary edge that begins in the 1830 treaty room.

Do not use several competing persistent devices.

## Behavior by room

### Hero
A faint trace may already be visible.

It should be perceptible only after one notices the architecture.

### 1830 treaty chamber
The member appears in its clearest original state.

It belongs naturally to this room.

### 1898
New identity machinery is added in relation to it.

The new structure may cross, touch, bracket, or partially obscure it.

It may not replace it.

### 1900
The administrative gate sequence becomes more segmented.

The treaty member remains visible through or behind the segmentation.

### 1901
The proposed wing is dashed and unrealized.

The treaty member passes through the chamber because the treaty remains real even where the proposed later structure did not become operative.

### 1902
The evidentiary architecture builds around the surviving member.

This is where the visual distinction should become especially clear:

**evidence carrier != origin node**

### MCR 879 review-authority void
The treaty member may remain present while the authority bridge is absent.

This is important.

A missing later bridge does not make the older structure disappear.

### Bell chambers
The member should still be detectable, but faintly.

It should not visually join the two Bell bodies.

It is shared legal ancestry of the administrative problem, not a person-identity connector.

## Prohibitions

The persistent member must not read as:

- chronology;
- historical causation;
- route;
- descent line;
- Robert-to-Robert identity line;
- progress indicator.

It is **structural persistence only**.

No widening in pass 2.

- material family;
- scale family;
- the faint persistent treaty member in the deep background.

No line between Roberts.

# VII. GOVERNMENT LAYER — PASS 2 LIMIT

A toggleable government-characterization layer remains a good later experiment.

Do **not** build the full toggle in this pass unless it emerges with almost no additional complexity.

For pass 2, it is enough to make government characterization visually typable:

- distinct border / material;
- explicit label;
- attributed language;
- visually removable in principle.

The experiment should prepare for a later on/off government layer without requiring it now.

# VIII. MOBILE IS A PRIMARY STAGE

Pass 2 must be judged on an actual phone-sized viewport, not only responsive CSS theory.

## Reading floor

A visitor should not need:

- browser zoom;
- reader mode;
- glasses they do not otherwise need for ordinary mobile reading.

If ordinary copy is smaller or harder to read than typical Google / ChatGPT mobile body text, the experiment fails legibility.

## Mobile rhythm

Monumental display type is reserved for earned moments:

- hero;
- major room-state transitions;
- ending.

Do not let every room become monumental.

Vary scale so monumentality retains force.

## 1901 mobile test

The phone view must clearly read:

**annotation → unbuilt room → door → unrealized continuation**

without looking like:

**text → broken blank area → tiny door → stray lines**

## Mobile line test

Inspect:

- portrait width;
- Safari browser chrome present;
- normal text size;
- no zoom.

# X. LANGUAGE STANDARD

Do not simplify the intellectual register merely to make the page more broadly familiar.

The workshop has earned precise language.

Preserve terms such as:

- identity determination;
- rule of evidence;
- statutory jurisdiction;
- administrative characterization;
- settlement / proof / enrollment;
- source-controlled;
- unrecovered authority;
- evidentiary carrier.

But enforce this distinction:

> **demanding language is allowed; illegible presentation is not.**

Make difficult ideas readable.

Do not make precise ideas vague.

---

# XI. PASS-2 STOP CONDITIONS

Stop the build when all five are true:

1. the 1901 door room reads as intentionally composed on phone and desktop;
2. every architectural line has an identified job;
3. one treaty structural member persists visibly from 1830 into later rooms;
4. ordinary copy remains comfortably readable on phone;
5. no new room, identity join, legal conclusion, or chronology had to be invented to make the design work.

Do not add more content merely because the implementation machinery is open.

---

# XII. ACCEPTANCE CHECK

Pass 2 succeeds if a visitor can plausibly feel all of the following before reading every note:

- there was an older legal room;
- later machinery was constructed around it;
- some later architecture became operative and some did not;
- evidence is not the same thing as the origin of a right;
- an administrative path can be visible while one of its authority bridges remains missing;
- the two Bell bodies are being compared without being merged;
- source distinctions survive the visual experience.

If the visitor instead mainly sees:

- a timeline;
- a legal infographic;
- a flowchart;
- decorative architectural lines;
- a two-Roberts theory page;

the pass has failed.

---

# XIII. ONE-SENTENCE BUILD INSTRUCTION

> **Clean the room, discipline the lines, and let one piece of the 1830 structure survive visibly through everything built after it.**
