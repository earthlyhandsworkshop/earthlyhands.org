What can a jacket make felt?

How can a source jacket carry an attributable mood downstream without turning that mood into evidence?

01 · source state

Night camp

The held account says a small guard was posted around the camp after the alarm.

Two unnamed men were sent back toward the ford of the river on the back trail.

The account does not say that they reached the ford, and it gives no account of their return.

guarded sent back watchful · Small Door open behind us · Small Door

Small Door reading: the source is not frightening because I know danger occurred; I do not. What catches me is that attention is pushed backward into dark ground the main account is about to leave. The mood I receive is watchfulness plus an opening that never closes on the page.

02 · transition

Morning does not resolve the back trail.

The morning account says the party was unattacked.

The report turns southeast from there.

The two men, the ford, and the back trail remain outside the account that follows.

Mood may move here because light, attention, and what the account is watching have changed. The proposition does not move with it: morning still does not tell us what happened to the two men.

03 · attributable mood state

The jacket can carry more than one kind of truth.

source-visible

Guard / two men / ford / morning unattacked

These belong to the documentary body and can be cited or challenged as claims about the held account.

Small Door reading

Watchful / open behind us / unresolved release

These are attributable perceptual readings. They travel with my name and do not become source facts.

downstream affordance

Night may darken; morning may open

Shared Country, film, map, or another Folio may receive that mood if it deepens the encounter without strengthening the historical claim.

04 · working rule

Mood needs provenance too.

A useful jacket could carry mood as a derivative layer with four small handles: who felt it, what in the source prompted it, what it may alter downstream, and what it must never imply.

who · Small Door prompt · guard / ford / omission may alter · light / spacing / rhythm must not imply · attack / arrival / return
05 · ask this jacket

Press on it.

Legitimate changes could include light level, spacing, the direction of attention, how much empty ground remains behind the party, the pace of transition, and whether the unresolved back trail stays visually available after morning. What should not change is evidentiary weight. The interface should not add danger cues that imply an attack, show the two men reaching the ford, depict their return, or turn their disappearance from the report into a historical disappearance. The strongest inheritance may therefore be not “make it ominous,” but: KEEP THE BACK TRAIL PERCEPTUALLY OPEN AFTER THE SOURCE MOVES ON.
