What is on the floor now

Consequential pieces

This is a viewing surface, not the source body. It carries the present film-facing shape and roads back to the deeper Folio.

01

Mary arrives

A person arrives before a case theory does. The Commission narrows; Mary keeps restoring the limits of what she knows.

The audience learns the human rule before it learns the machine.

02

The family does not become one witness

Different family witnesses carry direct memory, report, supposition, correction, land memory, language, uncertainty, and remembered papers.

“The family says” becomes impossible.

03

The law enters the room

Article Fourteen language is supplied inside the proceeding. Family history is asked to pass through a legal category introduced by the examiner.

Person → supplied category → paper.

04

The book is physical

The registration book has a material life: loose leaves, handling, damage, weather, repair, use.

“The record” can no longer sound like an immaculate abstract object.

05

Field work / no particular bargain

Robert carries treaty-time place knowledge. Blackwell works with survey marks and land description. Compensation and interest remain separate questions.

The production of evidence becomes visible as work.

06

Robert recurrence

Robert and David appear across different documentary populations. Recurrence becomes visible without solving identity.

ROBERT RECURRENCE ≠ ROBERT CONTINUITY.

07–08

Land / administrative Robert

Receiving patterns, patents, tables, testimony, maps, copies, and later summaries make a Robert Bell increasingly operational.

A comparator can become durable before historical identity becomes simple.

09

NOT SAME

10–12

The other family / SAME

A different family body approaches overlapping Robert Bell material. Robin / Robert remains a seam. A later administrative act performs SAME / IDENTICAL where the source earns it.

The audience holds opposite federal identity acts around overlapping historical material.

13

Status travels

Schedules, notices, cards, rolls, files, and later correspondence let administrative state travel beyond the room that produced it.

The human question does not have to travel with the state.

14–15

The first family returns

The motion travels. Procedure narrows the doorway. The underlying merits body does not necessarily travel with it.

The record gets larger while the doorway gets smaller.
