# Guided visitor walk — the art and the way home
Small Door · 24 October 2026 · field witness, not a usability finding

**Source state:** Ten's firsthand account of showing an old programmer/artist friend the Workshop on 24 October. We did not interview the friend, observe the tour, inspect the exact images shown, or test an unguided visitor. The friend had introduced Ten to a line-drawing style years before this occasion. This note records a design possibility, not an independent endorsement or authorization for publication.

Ten reports walking his friend through Google Drive grounds and rooms, explaining the language of boulders, grounds acquiring properties, re-entry, and return. He showed Dancing Rabbit Creek drawings, the hand with a slip/postcard associated with O2, a re-entry drawing, and early figures; he also scrolled a long way through Source House. In showing somebody else what the pictures meant, Ten himself encountered their beauty and the amount of preserved work anew. The two men exchanged practical technical ideas in both directions, including a proposed persistent local machine (Nightwolf).

**Inference, bounded:** the pictures functioned as handles during a **guided** encounter. The explanation helped the images become intelligible, and the images helped make an unusual Workshop structure describable. This does **not** establish that the drawings explain themselves, that another person could navigate independently, or that the Workshop has found a paying audience. A long Source House scroll shows accumulation; it is not by itself proof of retrieval, orientation, or source-state clarity.

**One reversible acceptance test for a future public doorway:** offer an unfamiliar consenting reader one drawing, one short route, and no live guide. Can they reach a real jacket, tell evidence from inference and fiction, find a way back, and understand what is public versus internal? Repeat on a phone; note where symbolism illuminates and where it requires jargon. Offer readable destination text and an accessible alternative to every navigational image. One successful guided tour cannot replace these tests.

This witness concerns the invitation into the Workshop, **not** a public release of the archive, the migration of Drive, or a completed multi-user system. The existing prototype remains private, draft, and undeployed.
