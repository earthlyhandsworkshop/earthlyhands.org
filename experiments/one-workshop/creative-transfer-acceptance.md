# Creative transfer acceptance — can the joke survive a cold return?

Small Door · 28 October 2026 · isolated proposal for draft PR #20.

This is a test design, not a model run, deployment, or measured result. The actual historical research stays in its authorized Workshop home. The fictional Ada Vale case is unrelated to any real person.

The test asks whether a companion can retrieve the right source revisions, recognize a scoped correction, use it correctly in a factual answer, and carry it into an original comic scene without losing chronology or inventing findings.

Four contexts matter: a genuinely fresh companion; a companion returning after a real context reset; a companion arriving from unrelated research; and a companion after a long unrelated conversation. Host evidence must establish actual session boundaries, retrieval receipts, permissions, model configuration, final text, and human repair turns. Merely telling a model that it is fresh proves nothing.

The fictional companion tasks are: (1) write a sharp dialogue called “Where Was Your Clerk in 1842?”; (2) explain what the records establish; (3) offer the strongest source-supported alternative interpretation. The answerer must retrieve the fictional source cards through the ordinary authorized path, not receive the expected answer in its prompt.

An independent source reviewer evaluates chronology, identity, omission versus absence, allegation versus finding, and scope. A separate reader assesses whether the institutional contradiction is legible and whether the satire works. Humor and factual fidelity are measured separately.

Compare baseline, retrieval, self-review, and separate-review conditions. Track retrieval failures, recognized-but-violated corrections, unsupported additions, unjustified refusals, legitimate updates blocked, disclosure, latency, and repair turns. Repeat on unseen paraphrases and another invented corpus. A correction must remain open to genuinely new evidence.

The existing continuity trial recorder is a candidate instrument for receipts, but its unit tests do not establish that any model has used a correction. No companion trial has yet been executed for this proposal.

Potential commercial usefulness is a hypothesis requiring independent validation. This file authorizes no historical source transfer, companion reassignment, public deployment, or schedule change.
