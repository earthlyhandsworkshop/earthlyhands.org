first working pass · Fieldglass · Work-attached Folio loaded
Observed The possible value is not “a Folio” as a generic software object. The live thing is a capacity: serious distributed work can remain inheritable without requiring one person to serve as the message bus. The work carries its own current state, history, brakes, roads home, and bounded question. Why that could matter outside Earthly Hands Many groups already have documents, chat, task systems, archives, and AI. The harder problem is continuity: how another person or machine can re-enter consequential work without flattening provenance, confusing current with history, or making a human repeatedly reconstruct the state. This specimen is beginning to demonstrate a different answer to that problem. What is not yet earned No outside person has paid for this. No market has been demonstrated. Reduced burden inside Earthly Hands is evidence of usefulness here, not proof of external demand. The instrument should therefore keep asking for encounters rather than converting its own usefulness into a business claim. Next test Put this Folio in front of one real outside body of work and watch for one thing: does it reduce re-entry or message-bus burden enough that the person wants to keep using it? If yes, money becomes a later question with evidence beneath it. If no, we learn where the Workshop-specific value stops.
