McKinney Holderness · 1837 testimony

A public house on the road.

Holderness says, “I kept spirits at my public house on the road.” The testimony gives the place and the ordinary transaction. It does not give the address.

Stand with the occurrence. Open only what the testimony carries. The road is named as a relation, not recovered as a route.
