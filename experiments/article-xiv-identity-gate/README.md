# Article XIV — Identity Gate

**Status:** active visual-grammar experiment  
**Public path:** `/experiments/article-xiv-identity-gate/`  
**Ground:** Article XIV / paired-Robert identity machinery  
**Maker:** True North

## Core proposition

The interface must keep three things distinct even when later administrative records collapse them:

1. an administrative identity act;
2. a source-controlled bridge;
3. Workshop historical identity.

The central `IDENTICAL` card performs the administrative act. It is allowed to be visually powerful. It is not allowed to resolve a source bridge merely by being powerful.

## Preserve this branch

Do **not** tear this experiment apart in order to make the other Robert/family body fit it. This branch has earned its own composition.

The current direction works especially well for the family/witness body moving toward an administrative identity gate. A sister experiment may later reverse the chronology for the other-Robert body, where the older record comes first. The two experiments may share visual grammar without being forced into the same directional history.

## Two-page / two-state structure

### Page I — the machine

The first view is a complete one-page piece:

- family / witness record body;
- central administrative `IDENTICAL` machine;
- Commission / older-record body;
- connector lines;
- one fixed **Properties** instrument at the bottom.

The wide view should retain its poster composition. The narrow view must not become a generic responsive stack: connector intelligence should survive by re-forming vertically through the stacked composition.

### Page II — what remains unresolved

Page II is not visible at rest.

Pressing `IDENTICAL` opens/reveals the second page. Page II contains:

- unresolved proposition lines;
- a right-side **Key points** panel;
- the exact same **Properties** instrument at the bottom.

The initial Key points state is the administrative `IDENTICAL` proposition itself. Touching any unresolved proposition line changes the Key points panel in place and also updates Properties. The geometry should remain stable; information changes inside the instrument.

For now, that is enough. Do not widen Page II into a larger workflow merely because more actions are imaginable.

## Properties instrument

Properties is not filler copy. It is the fixed object-inspection instrument for both pages.

At rest it should read as an intentional control surface:

- **PROPERTIES** as the persistent label;
- a clear invitation such as **Touch a record object**;
- source-local properties revealed when an object is selected.

Properties may show, depending on the object:

- designation;
- date;
- place;
- source voice;
- carrier;
- interpreter / translation chain;
- certification;
- land description;
- administrative use;
- unresolved limit.

The Page I and Page II Properties panels should behave as one instrument: selecting an object or proposition updates both states consistently.

## Dibbin Marris

There should be **one Dibbin Marris presence**, not a second standalone Dibbin strip consuming more page.

The good Dibbin material belongs in the interactive object/Properties machinery.

The important carrier sequence is allowed to become visually machine-like:

- Commission question: **“What was her name?”**
- Dibbin Marris — witness utterance;
- Oscar Billey — Official Interpreter;
- R. S. Streit — reporter/certifier of the surviving English record;
- surviving English answer: **Robert Bell.**

Do not label the Commission itself as “her.” The word belongs only inside the quoted question.

Do not label the original utterance “Choctaw” unless the source itself earns that proposition for this exact event. “Witness utterance,” “spoken utterance,” or “original spoken form not recovered here” is acceptable where that is all the source supports.

The graphical point is transformation into an official carrier: a spoken answer passes through translation/certification machinery and emerges in the surviving record as **Robert Bell**. That machinery does not by itself settle the Robert/Robin person relation.

Useful certification detail from the removed standalone Dibbin block should be retained in Properties rather than repeated in a second full-width element.

## Unresolved proposition grammar

The unresolved lines are the subject of Page II, not a compact data table.

They should be generous, legible, friendly, and slightly strange rather than businesslike. In particular:

- **ROBERT ? ROBIN** deserves featured scale and spacing;
- `Yimma ? Susie` remains a distinct relation question;
- `694.96 acres ? about 160 acres` remains a land-continuity question;
- `Section 4 ? Noxapater` remains a place/parcel question;
- language propositions cannot cross an unresolved person relation.

Touching a proposition changes Key points to show:

- one source side;
- the other source side;
- what source or bridge would actually change the state;
- the present unresolved relation type.

## Key points

Key points is a stable explanatory rail on Page II.

Default after pressing `IDENTICAL`:

- visible `IDENTICAL` seal/state;
- administrative proposition;
- source state;
- Workshop question / limit.

After touching an unresolved line, the panel changes in place to that proposition. It should not become a giant utility panel or compete with the unresolved lines for visual weight.

## Controlled brakes

- Jane witness voice `Robin Bell` remains distinct from older-record / administrative `Robert Bell` unless an independent person bridge is earned.
- Jane witness voice `Susie Bell` remains distinct from older-record `Yimma`.
- Jane's `about 160 acres` remains distinct from the 1846 patent's `694.96 acres` until land continuity is recovered.
- The older Robert's 1838 language evidence is source-local and cannot travel across an unresolved person relation.
- A Commission identity relation is displayed as an administrative act, not adopted as Workshop historical truth.
- Carrier machinery may be shown strongly; carrier machinery does not donate person identity.
- Field boundaries matter. Administrative address, company field, claimant field, witness utterance, interpretation, certification, and later agency use remain distinct propositions.

## Visual inheritance

Keep the things this experiment has already earned:

- the wide poster view;
- the connector / “spaghetti” lines;
- the family body and Commission body as visually distinct record populations;
- the central gate and `IDENTICAL` card;
- the bridge/unresolved material as a second movement rather than cluttering the first;
- one reusable Properties grammar across both movements;
- strong typography and generous spacing over dense dashboard behavior.

The narrow/mobile composition should preserve relations, not merely stack boxes.

## Future sister experiment

A later family-specific / other-Robert experiment may reverse the chronology:

**older record first → later family / descendant / Commission handling**

## Manual pairing before IDENTICAL

The upper record field now supports a deliberate temporary joke that teaches the rule.

Before the administrative card is pressed, a visitor may try to pair corresponding objects manually:

- Robin Bell ↔ Robert Bell
- Susie ↔ Yimma
- about 160 acres ↔ 694.96 acres
- Jane / Wash ↔ Section 4
- Dibbin Marris ↔ Murray–Vroom / patent

A manually attempted pair may hold together briefly, then releases/fades. It is a comparison attempt, not a durable identity relation.

Pressing **IDENTICAL** changes the state: the whole upper field becomes persistently selected and the connector field darkens with it. The interface is making a documentary joke, not a historical conclusion: the government administrative act can make predicates travel and stay together inside its record machinery even where the Workshop still leaves the source bridges open.

The lower unresolved movement should therefore remain open after IDENTICAL. The stronger upper visual join must never erase the lower questions.

## Lower-row source density

The expanded lower movement should use its existing footprint more fully rather than grow much taller. Each unresolved line may carry a restrained second line of source-local detail: date, place, carrier, role, or remembered land proposition.

Particular emphasis:

- **Noxapater / Neshoba County** must be visible in the land/place questions.
- **Susie** should carry Jane Bell's 23 March 1903 witness context.
- **Yimma** should carry the 13 May 1902 Office representation and the older record-book reference.
- **Murray–Vroom** should be dated as the **1837–38 Article XIV commission**. Do not mislabel that commission as 1842; later 1842 statutory machinery belongs to a separate layer.
