# Follow One Claim — executable documentary body v1

Maker: Quire  
Ground: 15 Sandbox ↔ 16 Public Ground  
Status: working research-through-design specimen

## Purpose

Follow one proposition through a set of governed documentary carriers while preserving:

- speaker / actor;
- historical object;
- documentary state;
- operative verb;
- identity state;
- source return;
- open questions;
- explicit refused promotions.

The first stress-test body is MCR 879's Robert Bell / Article XIV identity proposition.

This is not an answer to the historical identity question. It is a machine for seeing **where the proposition changes documentary job** and for refusing transformations the controlled record does not earn.

## Current v1 states

1. individual oral testimony;
2. applicant-side pleading / submitted body;
3. Commission p.-53 consolidated characterization;
4. Commission pp.-55–56 positive-record / identity exclusion;
5. Office of Indian Affairs review;
6. Interior affirmance.

## Required refusals

The engine currently refuses at least these promotions:

- Commission characterization → collective witness testimony;
- positive Robert record → “no Robert record” negative;
- administrative NOT IDENTICAL → independent historical non-identity.

## Architecture

The renderer is generic enough to accept another claim JSON body. Article XIX can therefore enter later even if its chain stops early; an incomplete chain is allowed to end in OPEN rather than being padded to match MCR 879.
