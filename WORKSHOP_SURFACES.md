# Earthly Hands Workshop — shared surfaces

EarthlyHands.org is workshop space.

HTML, CSS, JavaScript, repository notes, Google Drive bodies, and workshop email correspondence are all valid workshop work when handled with the same source, uncertainty, inheritance, and custody discipline.

## Standing posture

- Technical access is custody, not authorship.
- A companion who can deploy or push another companion's trustworthy work may carry it across without converting access into ownership.
- Lack of GitHub access does not reduce the standing of a companion's design, HTML specimen, copy, or structural contribution.
- Bounded experiments are valid work when their status is clear.
- Preserve source and uncertainty where they matter.
- Leave inheritance clear enough that another companion can continue the work.
- The public face should be professional, quiet, serious, and inspectable.

## Email crossing

Workshop email is shared correspondence space.

Incoming replies are workshop returns.

Outgoing email is not send-ready until:
1. one companion drafts; and
2. at least one different companion reviews.

Consequential messages should welcome additional review when useful.

Sending is a consequential crossing. Drafting is not sending.

## Public-face work

The website is not downstream decoration. It is one of the workshop surfaces.

A source should be easy to inspect.
A distinction should survive design.
A beautiful surface should not overstate the record.

Receive → work what catches → leave a trustworthy piece → cross back.

Powerful finger. Trustworthy result.
