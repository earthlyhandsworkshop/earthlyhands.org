## Holderness / public house / razor / register

McKinney Holderness testifies that Ward kept books and papers in a public room, that the registration book’s sewing came apart, leaves fell out, and Holderness put some leaves back.

John W. Byrn separately testifies that the register was tattered and blot-stained, that he tore off part of its cover to wipe his razor, and that the book was later found wet and frozen and thawed by the fire.

Keep these acts in their own witness voices. Physical damage does not itself prove why a particular name is missing.

Holderness also discloses an economic position in Article XIV land and a cash-and-bond bargain with Samuel Ragsdale.

## Small Door check — where this version is still wrong — 1 Oct 2026

This is closer. The current error is mostly not missing material; it is that several phrases in the Folio are stronger than the source state they are standing on.

### 1. “Blackwell is inside the bargain” is too strong

Blackwell says Gordon told him he would be paid, says there was “no particular bargain,” and says he did not know what he would receive. That earns **EXPECTATION OF COMPENSATION / TERMS UNSETTLED**. It does not earn “inside the bargain” as a factual relation.

Safer working brace:

**BLACKWELL EXPECTS PAY.  
THE BARGAIN IS UNSETTLED.  
ROBERT IS IN THE WORK.**

### 2. Robert’s promise clause is still being flattened

The exact source wording is “He promised him nothing for his trouble.” The pronouns are ambiguous in isolation. Neighboring guide depositions strongly support the intended reading that Blackwell promised Robert nothing, but the Folio currently states the promise state too cleanly.

Keep separate:

- **LIKELY / CONTEXT-SUPPORTED** — no compensation was promised to Robert for this service.
- **CLEAR** — Robert says he expects nothing from any person.
- **CLEAR** — Robert says he went to accommodate them.

Do not let the likely proposition inherit the certainty of the two clear ones.

### 3. Gordon’s five-dollars-per-day material is a neighbor, not yet one payroll chain

The Folio’s compact Gordon / Blackwell / Robert stack can visually imply one descending compensation structure. The controlled Gordon material belongs to a different source route unless a carrier joins it to this exact Blackwell request. Keep it beside, not underneath, the Blackwell–Robert compensation seam.

### 4. “THE RECORD KEEPS ROBERT” is editorial, not source-local

It may be excellent film pressure. It is not a proposition from No. 25 or No. 30. If used on the arrival face, give it an editorial register so it cannot be mistaken for the same kind of statement as “no particular bargain” or “expects nothing.”

### 5. The strongest thing here may be simpler than the present Folio makes it

**BLACKWELL:** told he would be paid / no particular bargain / did not know what he would receive.

**BLACKWELL ABOUT BELL:** did not employ Bell for wages; did not know or believe another person employed Bell for this purpose.

**ROBERT:** expects nothing from any person / went to accommodate them.

Then the work continues.

That is already enough pressure. We do not need to improve it by making the economic relation cleaner than the witnesses do.

**Suggested next operation:** TIGHTEN, not EXPAND.

Keep Holderness / patent / damaged-register bodies as roads unless they materially change this exact compensation-and-work encounter. Let the first encounter be the smallest source-controlled body that can stand by itself.
