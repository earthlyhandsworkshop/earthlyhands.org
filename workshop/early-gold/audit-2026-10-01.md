# Early Gold — rehoming audit

Date: 2026-10-01

## Standing

Early Gold preserves the Workshop's first articulated pieces and the history of how they were kept.

**Early** matters.  
**Gold** is provenance, not certification.

A piece may now have a better *present function* without losing its original body or history.

Do not clean the collection by moving everything out. Preserve the early occurrence. When a present use has matured, let the current rule, instrument, artwork, or working body live in its earned home and point back here.

## Audit rule

For each piece ask:

1. Does the original still matter as provenance?
2. Has its current practical function already migrated elsewhere?
3. Does a small present-day rule need extracting?
4. Is the piece itself becoming an artwork / interactive object / essay rather than infrastructure?
5. Is it better left alone?

**Do now:** when the source-method body is next touched, confirm that the current rule is already there before extracting anything. Do not duplicate blindly.

### A faithful inheritance does not transfer obligation

**Early Gold:** keep intact.

### Practice can precede principle

**Early Gold:** keep intact.

**Present function:** especially relevant to the Markdown migration and to the distinction between historical artifact and current canonical responsibility.

### What disappears during the crossing

**Early Gold:** keep intact and private in its present custody.

**Present function:** potentially a major research / art body about transformation, omission, administrative compression, AI mediation, and the difference between what enters a system and what survives its output.

**Art possibility:** very strong, but materially larger and more intimate than the other pieces.

**Do now:** do not copy its full content into the public repository. Do not reduce it to a Workshop rule. If it wakes again, treat it as its own bounded project / artwork and decide privacy, source, and audience at that time.

---

### The creek runs past the map

**Early Gold:** keep intact.

**Present function:** not infrastructure. It is already close to a complete small artwork / postcard / Polaroid.

**Art possibility:** very strong. Its power comes from restraint: the creek simply continues beyond the written Workshop map.

**Do now:** leave it intact. If a public or artistic ground later wants it, let that encounter earn a derivative form rather than converting the original into architecture.

## Collection-level result

The collection does **not** need evacuation.

Most pieces have already done their architectural work. Their current descendants live elsewhere.

Early Gold's new job is therefore simple:

**preserve the encounters that taught the Workshop how to become itself.**

Present practice should cite or inherit from them without making them carry daily governance.

Some pieces may later become art. That is not promotion above the others. It is a change of form earned by the piece itself.
