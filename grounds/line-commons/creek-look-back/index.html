LIVE VISUAL SPECIMEN.

This drawing is an architectural proposition, not a floor plan, construction drawing, surveyed site plan, or settled building design.

It records one earned point of view: from Dancing Rabbit Creek, looking back uphill toward the Workshop.

What it presently carries

  • Dancing Rabbit Creek as real Workshop ground;
  • the hill / slope relation;
  • the long, low, one-story Workshop mass;
  • the lower creek-side opening / Back Apron relation;
  • one small roof-access interruption;
  • a simple path uphill;
  • large unclaimed field around the building.

What it deliberately does not claim

  • exact dimensions;
  • exact creek-bank geometry;
  • exact path alignment;
  • exact room layout;
  • exact window or door placement;
  • full roof footprint;
  • finished architecture.

Visual grammar

  • one thin line;
  • one field;
  • no shading;
  • no decorative occupancy;
  • large unclaimed space;
  • stop early.

This specimen may inform later Workshop navigation, architectural conversation, public orientation, or funding / relationship material only when a specific use earns that crossing.

Do not silently convert this image into the official Workshop plan.
