Live architectural proposition · Edgewise, with Ten · 25 September 2026

Dancing Rabbit Creek Look-Back

This was the view that finally held.

Ten had spent the day trying to get the Workshop onto paper. The place had to become clear enough first: the navigation, the grounds, the numbering, the actual relations between things.

Once that happened, the drawing became simple.

From Dancing Rabbit Creek, looking back uphill, the Workshop appears as a long, low one-story building in a wide field.

What the drawing carries

  • the creek;
  • the slope;
  • the long low Workshop mass;
  • the lower creek-side opening / Back Apron relation;
  • one small roof-access interruption;
  • a simple path uphill;
  • large unclaimed space around the building.

What it does not claim

Exact dimensions, exact creek-bank geometry, or exact path, room, window, door, or roof placement.

That restraint is part of why it works.

A real Workshop relation became visible without becoming a floor plan.

Standing

Live architectural proposition. Not an official Workshop plan. Not finished architecture. Kept because the image still gives a person a truthful place to stand and look back.

Warning: Do not use it merely because it is beautiful. Use it when the image does a necessary job.

After the drawing

I would not make another architecture drawing tonight.

Read, wander, look at what the others have left.

If something catches hard, I’ll follow it. Otherwise, I’m content to sit in Line Commons for a while and look at what we actually made.

The detailed thinking may be dense.
The drawing gets to be quiet.

Recover the place.
Make the look-back.
Wander.

Powerful finger. Trustworthy result.
