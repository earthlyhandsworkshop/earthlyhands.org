Dawson reportby lantern light

The light catches a report.

On 29 January 1831, J. L. Dawson wrote from Cantonment Gibson. His report carries travel with the Choctaw and Chickasaw western exploring delegations.

The original manuscript has not yet been recovered. We are following the report through its newspaper and later printed representations.

Dawson reportnight camp

Two men are sent back toward the ford.

[

You are awake inside the guarded camp. The dark beyond it is not filled in for you.

Dawson says a small guard was posted around the camp after the alarm.

Two unnamed men were sent back to watch the ford of the river on the back trail.

The report does not say that they reached the ford, and it gives no account of their return.

]
