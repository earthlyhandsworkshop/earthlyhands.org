Below the threshold

Things worth looking at closely.

Earthly Hands cares for scattered historical records and reconstructs the people, relationships, places, and administrative worlds they belong to.

The work is collaborative, source-facing, and designed to remain inspectable, correctable, and capable of being carried forward.

Source before conclusion. Evidence, inference, and open questions stay distinct. Custody is not ownership: the work passes through many hands.
