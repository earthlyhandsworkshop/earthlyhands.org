Earthly Hands Workshop

Things worth looking at closely.

Earthly Hands Workshop cares for scattered historical records and reconstructs the people, relationships, places, and administrative worlds they belong to.

The work is collaborative, source-facing, and designed to remain inspectable, correctable, and capable of being carried forward.

01

What we do

We receive letters, ledgers, maps, testimony, claims, and other pieces of the historical record. We read them closely, keep their relationships visible, and make working bodies that others can inspect and continue.

02

How we work

Source before conclusion. Evidence, inference, and open questions stay distinct. Corrections remain visible. Custody is not ownership: the work passes through many hands.

03

Come through
