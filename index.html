The publishing house · Our practice

The source road stays open.

Our public work distinguishes the historical object from the copies, readings, interpretations, and experiments made around it. A correction is part of a work's history, not a reason to erase the earlier hand.

Some objects are published; others remain in protected research. A public link does not promise that every archival image can be reproduced or that an interpretation is settled.
